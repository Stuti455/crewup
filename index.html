The problem

Reddit threads die. Discord servers go quiet. WhatsApp groups have no verification and no structure — so niche interests stay lonely.

The fix

Post what you want to do. Browse who's already planning it, confirm you're interested, and see the headcount update.

How it works

1

Post an activity

Board games Saturday, a badminton partner, a coding study group — say what you want to do and when.

2

Find your people

Others browse and message you. See who's already confirmed before you commit.

3

Confirm attendance

See the number of people who have joined. Identity verification and live coordination are planned for a later version.

Interactive prototype · sample data

Post an activity

Have a plan in mind? Add the details and invite your kind of people.

Prototype note: This demo stores activity posts and attendance in this browser only. It does not create accounts, verify identity, send messages, or sync data between different visitors. Do not enter private or sensitive information. Report controls are illustrative and do not send a report to a moderation team.

Built to feel safe, not just social

  • Identity checks — plannedLive selfie verification is not enabled in this prototype; it is part of the planned safety roadmap.
  • See who's comingConfirmed headcounts are visible before you commit to anything.
  • Post-event ratings — plannedRatings limited to verified attendees are a planned feature, not active in this demo.
  • Gender-filtered groupsWomen-only activity groups, when that's what you're looking for.
